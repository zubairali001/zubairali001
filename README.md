## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Zubair Ali**, a **Software Engineer, Flutter Developer, and Unity Game Developer** passionate about building high-quality digital products across mobile, gaming, and web platforms.

My core expertise lies in **Flutter** and **Unity**, where I enjoy transforming ideas into polished, interactive, and production-ready experiences.

With Flutter, I work on modern cross-platform applications involving authentication, APIs, Firebase, subscriptions, analytics, ads, state management, and scalable application architecture.

With Unity, I develop both **2D and 3D games**, working with gameplay systems, player mechanics, AI, animations, physics, UI, monetization, performance optimization, and platform deployment.

I also have experience with the **MERN stack**, giving me a strong understanding of full-stack development and allowing me to work comfortably across frontend, backend, mobile, and game development.

I enjoy solving challenging engineering problems, improving user experiences, optimizing performance, and turning creative concepts into real products.
